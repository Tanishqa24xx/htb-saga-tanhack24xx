# My HTB Saga

*A running record of the path from student to penetration tester - one box, one challenge, one lesson at a time.*

---
 
## What This Is
 
This repository is my working log through HackTheBox — not a highlight reel, but the actual trail: what I attempted, what I broke, what I learned, and how my methodology sharpened along the way. It started as a journey through the **Pentester Job Path**, and has since grown to include everything else I work through on the platform — Machines, Challenges, Sherlocks, Pro Labs, and Fortresses.
 
I'm building this with one goal in mind: to become a working penetration tester who thinks clearly under pressure, documents rigorously, and never mistakes a shell for the finish line. Every entry here is a step toward that, written the way I'd want a teammate to read it — honest about what worked, honest about what didn't.

## How Each Writeup Is Built
 
I follow the same structure every time, because consistency is part of the methodology, not just the writing:
 
1. **Recon** — what I found, and how I found it
2. **Vulnerability Identification** — what the recon actually pointed to, and why
3. **Exploitation** — the path from finding to foothold to full compromise
4. **Remediation** — what I'd tell a client to fix, and why it matters
Screenshots of successful completions are included as proof of work — not step-by-step spoilers of active or ongoing content. Anything still live on HTB stays undocumented here until it's retired, in line with HTB's own guidelines.

## Why I'm Doing This Publicly
 
Because pentesting isn't just about getting the shell; it's about being able to explain, clearly and calmly, how you got there and what it means for the people whose systems you're testing. This repo is where I practice that second half of the job: the communication, the write-up, the "so what" after the exploit.
 
If you're reviewing this as part of an application or an interview — thank you for taking the time. What you're looking at is a work in progress, same as I am, and I'd rather show you the real trail than a polished highlight reel.
 
---
 
## A Note on Ethics
 
Everything documented here was completed in authorized lab environments on HackTheBox. No content from active/unretired machines, ongoing challenges, or live competition boxes is included. This repo exists to demonstrate learning and methodology — not to facilitate shortcuts for anyone else working through the same content.
